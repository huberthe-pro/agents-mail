# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We welcome contributors from all backgrounds. We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Standards

**Positive behaviors:**
- Be respectful and inclusive
- Welcome new contributors
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the community

**Negative behaviors:**
- Harassment of any kind
- Discriminatory language or actions
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information

## Enforcement

If you witness or experience violations, please open an issue or contact maintainers. We will address situations appropriately.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
